**Compact docks that leave the canvas available.**

- Keep detached docks sized to their controls, with a background only around the palette. The canvas continues behind them, without a blank full-height sidebar.
- Use compact desktop controls in floating docks. Choosing or dragging to **Top** restores the original single-row desktop toolbar, with no separate reset step.
- Identify the handle with a dotted grip and a visible **Move** label. Existing circuit-tool icons stay unchanged, and the lingering move tooltip is removed.
- Click, tap, keyboard-activate or start dragging **Move** to reveal all four docking destinations. The candidate under the pointer or focus highlights. Choose a destination, or cancel with Escape or an outside click.
- Adapt Fit, parts pickers, zoom and wire-action controls around the floating palette and phone navigation. Preserve the drawing and undo history while docking.

Requires Obsidian 1.14.0 or later. Circuit format and backup retention are unchanged. Automated phone checks use browser emulation; physical-device qualification remains separate.
