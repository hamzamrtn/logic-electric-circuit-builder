**Simulate your circuits, not just draw them.**

- Choose **Simulate** in the top bar to analyse the open circuit live. Simulate swaps the drawing tools for a run strip and docks an analysis panel below the schematic; **Build** returns to editing. Your file, save state and undo history are never changed by simulation.
- **Electrical circuits:** DC operating point and a power-on transient for passives, sources, diodes and LEDs, BJTs, MOSFETs, op-amps, comparators, switches, lamps, motors and meters. Wires are tinted by voltage, nets carry voltage tags, and hovering a part shows its voltage, current, power and state (V_BE/V_CE/I_C for transistors). The Scope plots probed nets and parts with two cursors and min/max/peak-to-peak/average/RMS/frequency for every trace.
- **Logic circuits:** four-state signals on every wire, tappable input switches, lit output probes and seven-segment displays, and an exhaustive truth table for up to 6 inputs.
- Clear diagnostics for unsupported parts, mixed logic/electrical circuits, feedback loops and driver conflicts, each with **Inspect**.
- Works on desktop, tablet and phone. Values use engineering notation such as `4k7`, `100nF` and `5V 1kHz`. Supported models and limits are listed in the [simulation guide](https://github.com/hamzamrtn/logic-electric-circuit-builder/blob/1.0.0/docs/simulation.md).

Requires Obsidian 1.14.0 or later. The circuit file format is unchanged. Simulation is a learning and analysis aid, not a substitute for SPICE sign-off or hardware testing; iOS has not been tested.
